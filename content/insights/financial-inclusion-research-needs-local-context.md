Financial inclusion is not only about access to accounts or mobile apps. It is also about trust, habits, language, informal systems, literacy, risk, and daily realities.

RediSerche can help finance and fintech teams collect grounded evidence from real communities, SMEs, households, agents, and customers.

## Research opportunities

- Financial behavior studies
- Credit and lending research
- Digital banking adoption
- SME finance research
- Customer trust and satisfaction
