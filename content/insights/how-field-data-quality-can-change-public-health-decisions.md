Public health decisions depend on the quality of the data collected from communities, patients, facilities, and households.

When fieldwork is weak, the resulting evidence can mislead programs and policies. RediSerche is designed to strengthen the chain from field officer recruitment to monitoring, consent, validation, and analysis.

## What stronger data quality can include

- Verified field officers
- GPS-enabled submissions
- Consent tracking
- Duplicate checks
- Enumerator performance monitoring
- Clean analysis workflows
