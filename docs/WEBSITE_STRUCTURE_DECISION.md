# Website Structure Decision

The website has been simplified into a cleaner standard information architecture.

## Main navigation

- Home
- About
- Platform
- Services
- Industries
- Grants
- Insights
- Contact

## Footer-only pages

- Payments
- Privacy
- Terms

## Why this is the better standard

A young company website should avoid too many thin pages. Each main page should feel complete, valuable, and credible. As RediSerche grows, individual service pages can be split out for SEO and sales campaigns.

## Current consolidation

- Research services, data analysis consultancy, health/CRO-style support, and grant implementation support are grouped under `/services`.
- Field officers are introduced under `/platform` and can later become a separate acquisition landing page.
- Payments is intentionally kept in the footer, not the main navigation.
- Insights is CMS-powered and can support blog posts, reports, news, case studies, and thought leadership.

## Future expansion

Separate pages can later be created for:

- Market research
- Public health research
- Data analysis consultancy
- Clinical research support
- Field officer recruitment
- Participant recruitment
- Baseline/endline studies
- Financial inclusion research
- Agriculture research
- Government and policy research
